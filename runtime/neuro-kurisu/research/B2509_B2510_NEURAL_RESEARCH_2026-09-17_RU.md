# Research synthesis — B2509/B2510

## 1. Actionable world models
Recent 2026 work argues that embodied world models should be judged by whether their predictions improve intervention, planning, learning, verification and recovery, not merely visual fidelity. This supports making B2509/B2504 outcome-coupled and measurable in closed loop.

## 2. World Action Models
Recent World Action Model surveys emphasize joint predictive state/action modeling and intervention-conditioned futures. B2509 therefore predicts internal body consequences conditioned on high-level actions rather than only classifying the current state.

## 3. Homeostatic reinforcement learning
A 2025/2026 line of work links reinforcement learning to homeostatic drive reduction and anticipatory regulation. B2509 incorporates a bounded homeostatic-relief objective so that action utility can include internal-state regulation rather than external reward alone.

## 4. Replay/consolidation
Sleep-like replay and recent continual-learning work support separating online learning from consolidation. B2510 applies the same multi-timescale principle to personality development: fast adaptation is filtered through evidence and periodically consolidated into slower traits, with an anchor to reduce uncontrolled identity drift.

## 5. Project implication
The most promising next architecture is a shared latent space in which body, self, world, memory and action consequences are co-predictive. The next experiments should measure transfer and recovery under sensor degradation, holdout environments and lesion conditions.
