# P2840 — Potential Impact Matrix

| Change | Expected impact | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| Shadow isolation | High | Internal world may become less active unless external observations are mapped back into world state |
| Evidence-weighted learning | High | Conservative learning may slow adaptation when verification is expensive |
| Divergence-triggered perception | Medium/High | Additional sensor cost on mobile |
| Combined mechanism | High | Requires long-horizon calibration to avoid excessive conservatism |

## Important interpretation

These are engineering hypotheses, not measured percentage improvements. Real gains must be established by repeated ablation and hardware benchmarks.
