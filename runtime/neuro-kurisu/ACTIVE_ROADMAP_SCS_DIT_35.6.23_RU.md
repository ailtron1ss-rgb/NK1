# Active Roadmap 35.6.23 — SCS/DIT

## Current architectural priority

**SCS/DIT = Self-Consistent State / Developmental Identity Trajectory**

The project should move from short-loop autonomy toward a stable life trajectory where verified experience can alter internal drives, autobiographical continuity, goals and future policy without an external script selecting the changes.

## Next large steps

### Phase 1 — Physical device closure
Real Termux sensors and real action execution. No host promotion.

### Phase 2 — Long-horizon embodied life
1000/5000/10000 cycles, restart, crash-resume, bounded memory, resource-aware degradation.

### Phase 3 — Autobiographical causal identity
Verified experiences become identity-relevant only when they repeatedly predict future behaviour and survive restart/holdout.

### Phase 4 — Endogenous goal ecology
Internal drives generate candidate goals, compete, persist, decay and are revised by verified consequences.

### Phase 5 — Personality trajectory
Traits change slowly from repeated verified experience, with anti-drift and counterfactual tests.

### Phase 6 — Functional consciousness experiments
Global workspace, recurrent broadcasting, metacognitive access and reportability can be tested. Phenomenal consciousness stays UNKNOWN.
