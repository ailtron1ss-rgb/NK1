## 10. Final audit conclusion

B400 is a genuine architectural progression over B390: the project now has a deeper integrated developmental layer connecting autobiographical memory, self continuity, goals, motivation, prediction, a lightweight plastic neural substrate, executive metacontrol and causal lesion testing. The integration is persisted in the canonical OrganismCore path and verified by independent regression/knockout/restart checks.

The most important remaining frontier is not adding more disconnected modules. It is making the neural substrate carry more of the autobiographical/self/predictive causal load while preserving evidence gating, one canonical decision owner, restart continuity and measurable unseen-context generalization.
