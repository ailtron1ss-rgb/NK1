# Adversarial Consciousness Research Protocol — 34.53.0

Research-only. No consciousness claim.

## Conditions
- control;
- treatment/intervention;
- sham intervention;
- lesion/ablation.

## Compare
- autonomy;
- action entropy;
- self prediction;
- identity continuity;
- workspace ignition;
- intrinsic goal rate;
- value revision.

## Interpretation discipline
A treatment effect is not sufficient. The protocol asks whether effects survive a sham condition and whether targeted lesion/ablation causes predicted functional loss and recovery.

## Scientific rationale
A 2025 adversarial collaboration comparing IIT and GNWT used preregistered divergent predictions, theory-neutral analysis, multimodal measurements and independent validation, illustrating why consciousness research benefits from explicit falsifiable contrasts rather than a single post-hoc score.
