# Два гигантских этапа после P25

## GIANT STEP A — P26 Neural Perception + Learned World Foundation

### Цель

Перевести P24 deterministic multimodal scaffold в настоящий learned perception/world layer.

### Пакеты

1. Semantic Vision Adapter: реальные vision backends, quality/latency/energy gates.
2. Semantic Audio Adapter: ASR + acoustic semantics с confidence/provenance.
3. Cross-Modal Latent Situation: единый scene state, temporal alignment и conflict resolution.
4. Learned Temporal World Model: sequence prediction, uncertainty calibration, residual learning и held-out evaluation.
5. Real Counterfactual Sandbox: executable simulated branches, alternative-action rollout и intervention ledger.
6. Perception Cache: mobile-friendly feature caching и thermal-aware inference.
7. Device-body model v2: battery/thermal/network/storage trajectory prediction.
8. Persistent identity/world linking: события мира становятся частью autobiographical evidence.

### Gate

- held-out prediction improvement;
- calibration improvement;
- multimodal conflict accuracy;
- latency/RAM/energy budget;
- no decorative semantic claims without real backend.

## GIANT STEP B — P27 Autonomous Research Organism + Longitudinal Self-Evolution

### Цель

Сформировать долговременно работающего исследовательского агента, который сам ставит проверяемые вопросы, проводит безопасные эксперименты и доказывает изменение поведения внешней оценкой.

### Пакеты

1. Research Campaign Planner.
2. Self-ablation scheduler.
3. Behavioral Genome evolution with lineage and rollback.
4. Model ecology: local/cloud specialist routing with empirical promotion.
5. Memory thermodynamics and temporal compression v2.
6. Energy-to-information economy.
7. Evidence Debt Market.
8. Cognitive Checksum + provenance graph.
9. Boredom-driven intrinsic experiment generator.
10. Longitudinal identity chapters and behavioral genome lineage.
11. Device endurance gate: 24h / 72h / 168h / 30d.
12. External evaluator packs with blind task sets.

### Gate

- measurable improvement over frozen baseline;
- no regression after model switch;
- bounded storage growth;
- bounded thermal/battery cost;
- reproducible replay;
- independent evidence for every strong capability claim.
