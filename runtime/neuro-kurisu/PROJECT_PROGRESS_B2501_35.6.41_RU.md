## Why the project still is not 100%

The organism has many functional subsystems, but several major scientific uncertainties remain. In particular, a working recurrent/spiking-like substrate, self-model, memory and metacognition do not by themselves establish phenomenal consciousness. Likewise, a successful simulated causal lesion is evidence about the architecture, not evidence that the system has human-like subjective experience.

## Decision rule for future upgrades

A candidate mechanism is promoted only if it improves the target metric without causing a material regression in the other protected dimensions. If one dimension improves and another drops substantially, the mechanism is rejected or redesigned. Small regressions of a few percentage points are acceptable only when the target improvement is large and independently verified.
