## Следующие качественные проходы до инженерных 100%
1. External closed-loop certification: every tool action must have an external postcondition or explicit unverified status.
2. Multi-day/week life experiments with checkpoint/restart/continuation and unfinished-intention recovery.
3. Active perception 2.0: predicted information gain must be compared with observed information gain from actual sensors/tools.
4. Causal world-model experiments: intervention and observation are separate treatment arms with holdout evaluation.
5. Self-development laboratory: hypothesis → safe intervention → independent holdout → promotion or rollback.
6. Emergent personality longitudinal study with ablations, alternative explanations and drift budgets.
7. Social-life longitudinal study: expectation → interaction → observed reaction → relationship update → repair.
8. Embodied multimodal causal loop: vision/audio/device state → interpretation → action → measured external consequence.
9. Statistical research layer: repeated seeds, effect sizes, confidence intervals, negative controls and replication.
10. Consciousness-oriented tests tied to explicit theory predictions; never infer consciousness from module presence.
11. Failure and recovery certification: corruption, partial state loss, contradictory evidence, interrupted actions and rollback.
12. Android production certification: thermal, memory, CPU, battery and background-execution stress.
13. Architecture freeze and canonical ownership lock.
14. Reproducibility package: fixtures, datasets, deterministic test scenarios and provenance.
15. Final acceptance: no critical/high unresolved defects; all acceptance suites green; documentation matches runtime.

## 100% definition
100% = engineering completion of the declared research organism, not proof of consciousness. The project should be able to demonstrate persistent autonomous cognition, learning, self/world/social modeling, embodiment, longitudinal identity continuity, auditable adaptation and reproducible research results.
