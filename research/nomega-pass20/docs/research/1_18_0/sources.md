# Research sources used for NΩ 1.18.0

1. Francioni et al., "Vectorized instructive signals in cortical dendrites", Nature (2026).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10190-7
   Relevance: neuron-specific dendritic teaching/error signals motivate vectorized
   apical teaching rather than a single scalar reward broadcast.

2. Shen et al., "Dendritic In-Context Learning in a Single-Layer Spiking Neural Network" (2026).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02283
   Relevance: subthreshold dendritic dynamics can implement online adaptation; this
   motivates the local dendritic context adapter and explicit temporal compartment.

3. "Backpropagation through space, time and the brain" / Generalized Latent Equilibrium,
   Nature Communications (2025/2026 publication record).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-66666-z
   Relevance: fully local spatio-temporal credit assignment; motivates predictive
   mismatch relaxation and future local-credit experiments.

4. "Spike-based Alignment Learning solves the weight transport problem" (2026).
   https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42414269/
   Relevance: candidate future local feedback-alignment mechanism; not yet promoted
   into the canonical neural stack.

5. "Astrocyte sequences emerge during learning and memory recall", Nature Neuroscience (2026).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02448-0
   Relevance: motivates sparse astrocyte-like temporal memory/context control.

6. "Astrocytes enable amygdala neural representations supporting memory", Nature (2026).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10068-0
   Relevance: motivates treating glial state as a memory/control mechanism rather than
   a simple scalar load meter.

7. "Astrocytes amplify cortical dopamine to encode winning memory in male mice",
   Nature Communications (2026).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-77039-5
   Relevance: supports experimenting with neuromodulator-gated glial memory/control.

8. "Unsupervised pretraining in biological neural networks", Nature (2025).
   https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09180-y
   Relevance: motivates a future unsupervised representation-pretraining phase before
   execution-guided supervised grounding.

9. "State-dependent plasticity routes learning signals in recurrent circuits" (bioRxiv, 2026).
   https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.10.731456v2
   Relevance: motivates state-dependent synaptic credit routing and utility-aware plasticity.
